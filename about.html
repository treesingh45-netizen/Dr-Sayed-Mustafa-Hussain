Your Trusted Specialist

Dr. Sayed Mustafa Hussain

BDS, FCPS (OMFS) · Certified Implantologist · Certified Orthodontist

Dr. Sayed Mustafa Hussain is a distinguished oral and maxillofacial surgeon who has dedicated his career to transforming smiles and improving the lives of his patients. With a degree in Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) and a prestigious Fellowship of the College of Physicians and Surgeons (FCPS) in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, he is one of the most qualified dental professionals in the region.

His extensive training in implantology and orthodontics allows him to offer a comprehensive range of dental services — from simple dental restorations to complex jaw surgeries and full-mouth rehabilitations with dental implants.

Dr. Hussain believes that every patient deserves personalized, compassionate, and evidence-based dental care. His approach combines cutting-edge technology with gentle techniques to ensure optimal outcomes and patient comfort.
